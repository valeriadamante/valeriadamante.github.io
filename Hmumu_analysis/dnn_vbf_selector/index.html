    The analysis DNN and the VBF selector¶

    7 October 2026. The full note, with figures, is the PDF dnn_vbf_selector.pdf. Its LaTeX source is in dnn_vbf_selector/; to rebuild it, run pdflatex dnn_vbf_selector.tex twice in that folder. This page summarises the note and keeps the fit plan in a form that is easy to update.

    The two networks¶

    analysis DNN (DNN_NNOutput) VBF selector (VBFSel_NNOutput)
    question is it H→μμ? is it a VBF topology?
    signal VBF H + ggH VBF H + EWK Zjj
    background DY, EWK Z, tt̄, VV (training) DY, ggH, tt̄, VV
    training region cut-based VBF category, Signal_Fit (115–135 GeV) baseline_ge2J, Z + H sidebands + Signal_Fit (inclusive_regions)
    dimuon mass input (m_mumu) not used
    inputs 32: muons, dimuon, Collins–Soper angles, tag jets, soft activity, era_code 24: four leading jets (pT, η, φ, QvG, CvL), pT(μμ), N jets, soft activity
    network 32→86×4→1, ReLU, no regularisation 24→100×4→50×2→1, BatchNorm + ReLU + dropout 0.1
    training 2022–2025, abs weights, classes equalised, 4 folds 2022–2026, signed weights, background capped per process/region/era, classes equalised, 4 folds
    optimiser Adam lr 1e-3, batch 10k, epochs = 1 in the deployed config Adam lr 5e-3, batch 10k, early stopping (patience 10)
    payload common/updated_DNN_{configs,models} common/vbfsel_{configs,models}
    typical AUC 0.89–0.91 (SR, VBF) 0.90 (Z sideband), 0.82 (H sideband, SR)
    use fit variable of the H→μμ fit (SR + H sideband, 14 bins per era) EWK Z fit in the sidebands; possible alternative VBF category

    Both networks are applied in the framework in the same way: - each event is scored by model FullEventId % 4, so never by a model that saw it in training; - the input standardisation is inside the ONNX graph; - the score is re-evaluated for every JES, JER and muon scale/resolution variation (dnn_application.ApplyDNNVariations, since 29 September 2026).

    The production DNN was trained outside this repository (A. Yeagle, OGDNN_big_run). The VBF selector was trained here, in studies/vbf_selector/.

    Results to date¶

    • H→μμ, DNN fit. Expected significance 2.10σ (toysFreq Asimov, SR + H sideband, 7 eras, DY lnn), 2.48 with statistics only. These numbers use the old dnn_vbf_weighted templates, whose jet and muon systematics are invalid, and have to be redone. Details: combine/RESULTS.md.
    • EWK Z normalisation. If it is left free, the expected significance drops from 2.14 to 1.63: this is the reason for the VBF selector (why).
    • VBF split CC/CF/FF. +1.8% (2.366 → 2.410), without shape systematics.
    • VBF selector.
    • AUC 0.90 in the Z sideband and 0.82 in the H sideband and SR; about 0.855 in 2022–23 and 0.815 in 2024–26, which follows the horn veto.
    • SHAP: it is a classifier of the tag-jet kinematics.
    • As a VBF category (counting): 1.105 against 1.014 for the cut-based category; the gain comes from the ggF side.
    • EWK Z in the sidebands. r = 1.051 ± 0.033 (DY Hard/PU, no shape systematics). This is not yet a measurement: the eras range from 0.62 to 1.27, the Z and H sidebands give 1.02 and 1.70, and the DY shape is mismodelled.

    Fit plan¶

    The state is as of 8 October 2026, 00:45.

    • Order. Everything is done inclusive first (VBF category not split). After a checkpoint, the CC/CF/FF split is run.
    • Templates. The DNN fit templates are dnn_vbf_eta_regions_syst, with incl for the inclusive category and CC/CF/FF for the split.
    • Automation. combine/results_fit_matrix/logs/chain_20261008e.sh (replaces chain_20261007d.sh): phase 1 rebuilds and validates the 2022-2025 templates and runs D7, D8, D12, V3 and V4 on 2022-2025; phase 2, when the 2026 MC is redone, repeats everything on the seven eras. Fits run only if tools/validate_variations.py finds no critical flag.
    • 2026 muon scale factors. The 2026 skims have none: since 8 October they are the 2025 ones, computed at histogram time (maincfg.yaml muon_sf_era), and the 2026 MC of the fit campaigns is being redone.
    • CC/CF/FF campaigns. Their 10 386 queued jobs were removed on 8 October; resubmit_eta_campaigns.sh resubmits them (missing outputs only) when the chain has finished.
    • DNN fit order. Significance and limits of every variant and DY model first (combine/DNN_FIT_RESULTS_<tag>.md), then the VBF-selector fits, then impacts, scans and the fit matrix (post-fit plots in the SR (blinded above 0.8) and in the H sideband).

    Order¶

    1. Rebuild and validate the inclusive templates.
    2. Run D7–D9.
    3. Run V3–V5.
    4. Checkpoint: report the inclusive results.
    5. Run D12–D14 and V6–V8.
    6. Run D15, then D16, D17 and V9.
