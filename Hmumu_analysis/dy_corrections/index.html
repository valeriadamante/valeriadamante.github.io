    TAMU ad-hoc DY corrections¶

    The TAMU analysis applies three data-driven corrections to the Drell--Yan (DY) simulation. They are derived in the Z mass sideband after subtracting the non-DY prediction and are evaluated sequentially: each stage is derived on top of the preceding correction.

    The payloads are produced for four statistically combined period groups:

    Payload period Applied to
    2022 + 2022EE 2022 and 2022EE
    2023 + 2023BPix 2023 and 2023BPix
    2024 2024
    2025 2025

    1. DY jet-component normalization¶

    This correction adjusts the relative normalization of DY events according to the reconstructed-jet multiplicity and the hard-scatter or pile-up origin of the jets.

    The exclusive components are:

    • 0 reconstructed jets;
    • 1 hard-scatter jet or 1 pile-up jet;
    • 2 hard-scatter jets, 1 hard + 1 pile-up jet, or 2 pile-up jets;
    • VBF hard-scatter and VBF pile-up components.

    The two-jet components are fitted first using the subleading-jet η and transverse momentum. The one-jet components are then fitted in the exclusive one-jet data region using the leading-jet distribution, and finally the zero-jet normalization is fitted in the dimuon-mass distribution. An independent fit is performed in the VBF control region.

    The fit is a binned χ² minimization with non-negative normalization parameters. Parameter uncertainties and correlations are obtained from the inverse-Hessian covariance matrix.

    2. Dilepton-pT correction¶

    The $p_T(Z)$ correction uses the reconstructed dimuon transverse momentum, $p_T(\mu\mu)$. It is derived separately in four categories:

    • ggF with 0 selected jets;
    • ggF with 1 selected jet;
    • ggF with at least 2 selected jets;
    • VBF with at least 2 selected jets.

    In each category the starting ratio is

    $$ R(p_T)=\frac{N_{\mathrm{data}}-N_{\mathrm{non-DY}}}{N_{\mathrm{DY}}}. $$

    The ratio is fitted between 0 and 200 GeV with a smooth function consisting of a constant, two Gaussian terms and a falling-power term. The fitted function is stored as the nominal correction. VBF events with fewer than two selected jets receive unit weight.

    3. Reconstructed-jet multiplicity correction¶

    The NRecoJet correction adjusts the selected-jet multiplicity independently for the ggF and VBF categories. For every jet-count bin,

    $$ w(N_{\mathrm{jets}})= \frac{N_{\mathrm{data}}-N_{\mathrm{non-DY}}}{N_{\mathrm{DY}}}. $$

    Bins cover 0 through 9 selected jets, with an inclusive overflow bin for ten or more jets. Statistical uncertainties are propagated from data, non-DY and DY. Bins with insufficient DY yield fall back to unit weight, and values outside the tabulated range are clamped to the nearest valid bin.

    Derivation procedure¶

    The three corrections must be derived in this order:

    1. produce the Z-sideband inputs for the jet-component fit;
    2. merge the inputs and derive the component-normalization payload;
    3. reproduce the DY templates with that payload applied;
    4. derive and validate the $p_T(Z)$ correction;
    5. reproduce the DY templates with the first two corrections applied;
    6. derive and validate the NRecoJet correction;
    7. regenerate the final DY templates with all three corrections.

    The repository driver executes one incomplete stage at a time:

    ./hmumu dy-weights run --eras 2022,2022EE,2023,2023BPix,2024,2025,2026

    It either submits the first missing production stage or, when its inputs are available, merges them, performs the fit and validates the resulting payload. It should be called again after each batch wave until all three stages are up to date.

    For an individual period and correction stage, the explicit sequence is:

    ./hmumu dy-weights check         --weight-stage jet --eras 2024
./hmumu dy-weights submit        --weight-stage jet --eras 2024
./hmumu dy-weights hadd          --weight-stage jet --eras 2024
./hmumu dy-weights fit           --weight-stage jet --eras 2024
./hmumu dy-weights check-weights --weight-stage jet --eras 2024

    Replace jet with ptll or njets for the following stages. Each fit produces diagnostic comparisons before and after reweighting. A payload must be derived again whenever its input histograms change; all downstream DY templates must then be regenerated.

    Validation requirements¶

    Before adopting a new payload:

    • verify closure in every derivation category before and after reweighting;
    • inspect bins close to zero DY yield and bins hitting the allowed weight range;
    • check that the fitted $p_T(Z)$ function is smooth over the full interval;
    • confirm that the correction improves the Z-sideband agreement without sculpting unrelated observables;
    • repeat the validation independently for every payload period;
    • record the payload version together with the input production version.
