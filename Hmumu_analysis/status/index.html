    H->mumu DNN fit¶

    Now (fit matrix, 2026-10-04). toysFreq Asimov (nuisances fitted to the data with r = 1, SR above DNN = 0.8 out of that fit) next to the pre-fit one, 7 eras, lnn: SR + HSB 2.102 (pre-fit 2.135), SR only 1.932 (1.964); rateparam-both 1.876 (1.991), inclusive 1.879 (2.029). toysFreq is lower by 1.5% (lnn) to 7% (free DY). Leading impact still QCD_fac_scale_V (0.127). Pre/post-fit plots of the data fit (Z and H sidebands, SR masked): fit_matrix/dnn_sr_hsb_zsb/. Details in combine/RESULTS.md, section "Now".

    On 2026-09-30. SR + H sideband, cut-based VBF category, 7 eras, pre-fit Asimov: lnn 2.130, rateparam-pu 2.089, rateparam-both 1.986; SR only 1.995. The old 2.23 (2 September) was inflated by a missing VBF-filtered DY in 2024-2025. The leading nuisance is QCD_fac_scale_V (0.144 on r), mostly from EWK Z at high DNN score: normalising EWK Z in the sidebands (VBF selector fit) is the plan. Statistics dominates: removing all constrained nuisances only reaches 2.51.

    VBF selector and EWK Z¶

    Now (fit matrix, 2026-10-04). Same r on data (1.051, Z 1.020, H 1.70); expected significance 41.9 pre-fit, 44.3 toysFreq; in the data fit the Z-sideband MC-stat parameters are pulled up to 7 sigma (2025): the DY shape is still mismodelled with the Hard/PU split. studies/vbf_selector/RESULTS.md, section 4.

    On 2026-09-30. A classifier of VBF H + EWK Z against DY, ggH, ttbar, VV, on 24 inputs without the dimuon mass; inclusive_regions is deployed as the VBFSel payload. As a VBF category (score > 0.96) it keeps 22% of VBF H and <1% of ggH; the counting gain (1.105 vs 1.014) comes from the ggF category, and the tight VBF category alone is worse. The EWK Z sideband fit with DY split into Hard and PU gives r = 1.051 +- 0.033 all eras, but the eras (0.62-1.27) and the sidebands (Z 1.02, H 1.70) disagree: DY shape and pile-up-jet mismodelling, not yet a measurement.

    Small DNN per definition (2024, Hmm_newSkim/combine/results_vbf_pair_definition/small_dnn/README.md, code in Hmm_newSkim/studies/vbf_pair_definition/small_dnn/): an MLP 64-32 on the 31 production inputs, retrained for each definition, 2-fold on the event parity, three seeds; same binning procedure and same SR + H sideband card (lnN only).

    mean of 3 seeds AUC (Signal_Fit) Z, lnn Z, lnn, stat only Z, rateparam-both
    max m(jj) 0.908 +- 0.001 1.440 +- 0.018 1.563 +- 0.019 1.125 +- 0.046
    hardest 0.906 +- 0.004 1.373 +- 0.062 1.499 +- 0.061 1.077 +- 0.096
    two leading 0.913 +- 0.001 1.414 +- 0.047 1.533 +- 0.047 1.162 +- 0.025
    • With a dedicated network the three definitions have the same sensitivity within the seed-to-seed spread (2-8%). The +2-4% of the alternatives with the production DNN came from applying a network trained on max m(jj).
    • Two leading always has the best AUC, with 21% less signal.
    • Sensitivity does not choose the definition: two leading wins on purity, pile-up and forward-jet population; hardest on accuracy and acceptance.
    • Keep the base cuts: tightening m(jj) above 400 GeV always loses with the DNN, tightening deta gains at most 0.6%.
    • Caveats: 2024 only; VV, W, single top and non-signal H (about 10% of the VBF background) are neither in the training nor in the card; a much smaller network than the production one. The small-DNN code and the study write-up are not yet in this repository.

    The larger lever is the forward-jet veto: in 2024-26, 58-63% of VBF DY has a pile-up VBF jet (16-21% in 2022-23), because the 2024-26 horn veto covers only 2.5 <= |eta| < 3.0 while the 2022-23 one covers every |eta| >= 2.5 below 50 GeV. Testing the 2022-23 veto on 2024-26 is not done yet.

    VBF split by tag-jet eta (CC/CF/FF)¶

    Binned Asimov Z on the DNN, statistics only, 2022-2025: inclusive 2.770, CC+CF+FF 2.889 (+4.3%; 2024 +4.0%, 2025 +5.6%). FF holds a fifth of the signal but as much sensitivity as CF, and the worst data/MC at DNN > 0.94 (+18% in 2024, +30% in 2025): it would need its own DY treatment. The Central-card check (stat + lumi + MC stat, SR + H sideband, 6 eras) gives +1.9% (2.236 -> 2.278). The +7.7% of 2026-09-25 (2.255 -> 2.429) came from the same cards and templates (identical workspaces, checked) significance computed with --toysFreq: the Asimov is then built after fitting the nuisances, here the Barlow-Beeston MC-statistics parameters, to the observed data, high-score SR included, and the FF excess inflates the split sensitivity. With the MC statistics frozen both Asimov types give +2.1% (2.348 -> 2.398 pre-fit, 2.358 -> 2.408 toysFreq). The combine version does not matter (v11.0.0 and v11.1.0 give 2.27803 on both workspaces). A free DY normalisation per era and eta category (common to SR and H sideband) keeps the gain: 2.225 -> 2.267 (+1.9%), the freedom costs 0.5%. With 2026 (produced on 2026-10-02), 7 eras: 2.350 -> 2.393 (+1.8%); 2026 alone 0.726 -> 0.732 (+0.8%). Now (2026-10-05), toysFreq with the SR out of the data fit (the default from now on), same 7-era cards: 2.366 -> 2.410, +1.8%: the +7.7% came entirely from the SR data in the one-line --toysFreq fit (eta_split_central/toysfreq_blinded/). Missing: full systematics (the dnn_vbf_eta_regions campaign is Central only, and --vbf-eta-regions excludes the DY Hard/PU split in hist_maker.py). Cards and logs: combine/results_signal_extended_7eras/eta_split_central/.

    Jet horn veto (2025, 2026)¶

    Without the veto the 2.5 < |eta| < 3.0 window is the worst-modelled part of the jet eta spectrum (horn/rest chi2 ratio 4-13, DY off by 10-30%), in 2026 as in 2025. No event is lost: 11% of VBF H and 30% of ggH move from VBF to ggF (6-7% of VBF H at DNN >= 0.9); counting S/sqrt(B) -2% in VBF, +1% in ggF. Keep the veto. Next: the combine significance with templates without the veto, and a pT-threshold scan (60, 80 GeV).

    Muon checks¶

    • SF on gen-matched muons only (same-sign W region): yields move by 0.2-1.3% (2022-2025); the region needs a factor 1.9-3.5 on MC because of fakes, so it cannot tell the options apart. 2026 not run.
    • BSC chi2 >= 30: plots of the affected fraction, m(mumu), muon pT and chi2 against gen truth for every era and main process. A table of the affected fractions by era and category is still missing.

    EWK Z generator m(jj) cut¶

    The EWK_2Mu2J 105-160 samples have LHE m(jj) > 120 GeV. Tightening it to 150 on 2024 removes 4.3% (herwig) / 4.2% (pythia) of the baseline EWK Z yield and 0.75% of the VBF one, so the generator cut hardly matters in the VBF category. studies/ewk_lhe_mjj/, output results/ewk_lhe_mjj/Run3_2024/lhe_mjj_summary.json.

    FlashSim¶

    The Electron_mvaIso_WP90 patch vetoed every electron above 20 GeV (fixed 2026-09-29): skims before the fix lose 2-15% of events, and the plots from the 17 September hadds are marked PRE_WP90_OLD_HADDS. The Sep2026 samples are being reproduced; their schema only allows jet and muon variables (no DNN, no soft activity). Open: trigger matching in herwig FlashSim (0.64 vs 0.79 FullSim), while pythia, DY and TT agree.

    Synchronisation¶

    • Pisa (Filippo), Run2024H data: 22 158 events (ours) vs 22 029; 21 984 common, 99.8% of his; 219 differ in the union. Before alignment the main mismatch was the VBF pair (he used j0, j1); the residuals are the veto-map treatment in b veto and VBF jets, 2024 JEC for 2.0 < |eta| < 2.5, the electron threshold and FSR recovery.
    • gpizzati v7, Run2024C: 99.5% of the data skim events common; common-event yield ratios 0.9999 (baseline Z sideband) and 1.0002 (VBF); VBF H powheg 0.9999 / 1.0004 in Signal_Fit. Only 44.5% of the MC events overlap (different extensions and lumi splits), so only the common-event comparison counts.
